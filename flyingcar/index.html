Sky Taxi Simulator Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026

In short: Sky Taxi Simulator does not collect, store on a server, sell or share your personal information. There are no accounts, ads, in-app purchases or analytics. Your game progress stays on your device.

Who this policy covers

This policy applies to Sky Taxi Simulator (the "Game"), published by LogicStack ("we", "us"), on Android devices and on Apple iPhone and iPad devices wherever the Game is offered.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information through the Game. The Game does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or payment details, and it has no sign-in or user account.

The riders, addresses, ratings, fares and cities in the Game are fictional. The Game does not use your real location.

Information saved on your device

So that you can continue where you left off, the Game saves the following on your device only:

  • Game progress, such as in-game earnings, trips, rider rating, taxi upgrades, paint colors, goals and achievements
  • Settings, such as your radio station and graphics options
  • The date and time of your last session, read from your device clock, which are used for daily goals and in-game earnings while you are away

This information is not sent to us, and we cannot see it. In-game money is fictional and has no real-world value.

Device permissions

PlatformWhat the Game uses
Android Vibration only, for short haptic effects during play. The Android version does not request internet access, so it cannot send data from your device.
iPhone and iPad No system permission prompts. Haptic effects do not require a permission. The Game does not track you and does not use Apple's advertising identifier.

On both platforms, the Game does not access your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files.

Third parties

The Game contains no advertising, analytics or social media services.

You download the Game through Google Play or the Apple App Store. Those stores process information about you and your device under their own policies, which we do not control:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Apple Privacy Policy

The stores may give us combined statistics such as download counts, and technical crash reports if you have chosen to share them. We use these only to understand and improve the Game.

Device backups

Depending on your device settings, saved game data may be included in backups you make with your Google or Apple account. Those backups are controlled by you and handled by Google or Apple.

Deleting your data

  • Use Reset progress in the Game's settings to erase your game progress.
  • Uninstall the Game to remove everything it has saved on your device.

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete on our side, unless you have emailed us. If you have, you can ask us to delete that correspondence.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country).

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA may give you rights to access, correct or delete your personal information. Because the Game does not collect personal information, we normally hold nothing to act on. If you contact us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you, and you may exercise these rights over that correspondence.

Changes to this policy

If the Game changes in a way that affects your privacy, such as by adding ads, analytics or online features, we will update this page and its effective date before that change is released.
