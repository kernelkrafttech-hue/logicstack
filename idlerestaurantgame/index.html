Privacy Policy

App: Idle Indian Restaurant game (com.logicstack.idleindianrestaurantgame)
Developer: LogicStack
Last updated: 20 July 2026

Summary

Idle Restaurant Game does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal or device data. The game runs fully offline. It has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and requires no network permissions.

Information we collect

None. We do not collect any personal information, device identifiers, location, contacts, usage analytics, crash reports, or advertising identifiers.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (such as coins, upgrades, and settings) is saved locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party. Uninstalling the app removes this local save data.

Third-party services

The app integrates no third-party SDKs that collect data — no advertising networks, no analytics providers, and no social logins.

Advertising

The app does not display third-party advertisements and does not use the Android Advertising ID.

Purchases

All in-game currency and rewards are earned through gameplay. The app does not offer real-money in-app purchases.

Children's privacy

The app is intended for players aged 13 and over. Because we collect no data at all, we collect no data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this app changes to collect any data in a future version, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be reflected on this page.
