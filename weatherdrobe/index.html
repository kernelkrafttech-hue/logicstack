Privacy Policy — WeatherDrobe

Last updated: 30 June 2026

WeatherDrobe ("the app", "we", "us") is a weather-and-wardrobe app that shows an outfit suited to today's weather. This policy explains what data the app handles and why. WeatherDrobe is a fully on-device app: we operate no servers, user accounts, or profiles, and we do not track you.

Summary

  • No accounts. No sign-in. No advertising. No analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • Your location is sent only to weather providers, only to fetch a forecast.
  • Photos you add stay on your device and are never uploaded.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored.

Information We Process

Location

To show local weather, the app may request your device location (precise or approximate). If you grant permission, your latitude and longitude are sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to our third-party weather providers (see below) solely to retrieve a forecast for that point. You can instead search for a city by name; that search text is sent to a geocoding service to resolve coordinates. Location data is used only for app functionality. It is never used for advertising or tracking, and we do not store it on any server of ours — we have none.

Photos

The app lets you pick or capture images for your wardrobe. These images are stored locally on your device only. They are never transmitted off the device, uploaded, or shared.

App Settings

Preferences (units, theme, notification choices, selected location) are saved locally on your device using standard on-device storage. They are not sent anywhere.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, notifications are scheduled and shown locally on your device. No notification data leaves your device.

Third-Party Services

The app sends weather and geocoding requests to the following providers. We share only the coordinates or city-name text needed to return a result:

  • Open-Meteo — weather forecasts and city search (open-meteo.com). See open-meteo.com for their terms.
  • The Weather Company / IBM — weather data (api.weather.com). See ibm.com/privacy for their policy.

These providers receive a location to answer a forecast request. Refer to their policies for how they handle request data.

Data Sharing and Sale

We do not sell your data. We do not share your data with anyone except the weather providers above, and only the minimum needed to fetch a forecast.

Data Security

All network requests use encrypted HTTPS connections. Because data stays on your device and we run no servers, there is no central store of your information to breach.

Data Retention and Deletion

All app data — settings and photos — lives only on your device. To delete it, uninstall the app. We hold no copy to delete on your behalf because we store nothing on our side.

Children's Privacy

WeatherDrobe is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Permissions We Request

  • Location — to fetch weather for where you are.
  • Camera / Photos — optional, to add wardrobe images (stored locally).
  • Notifications — to show local weather/outfit reminders.
  • Internet — to request forecasts.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
