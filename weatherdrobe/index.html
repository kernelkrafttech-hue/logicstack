Privacy Policy — WeatherDrobe

Last updated: 2 September 2026

WeatherDrobe ("the app", "we", "us") is a weather-and-wardrobe app that shows an outfit suited to today's weather. This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

It covers both versions of the app — the Android version distributed through Google Play, and the iPhone and iPad version distributed through the Apple App Store. The two are built from the same code and handle your data the same way. Where a platform genuinely differs — the store that takes a payment, the name of a permission, where you go to change a setting — this policy says so explicitly and names both.

WeatherDrobe is a fully on-device app: we operate no servers, user accounts, or profiles, and we do not track you.

Summary

  • No accounts. No sign-in. No advertising. No analytics SDK of our own, on either platform.
  • Your location is sent only to our weather provider, only to fetch a forecast.
  • Photos you add stay on your device and are never uploaded.
  • If the app crashes, an anonymous diagnostic report may be sent so we can fix it.
  • If you buy Premium, the purchase is handled by Google Play on Android or the Apple App Store on iPhone and iPad — whichever your device uses — and confirmed through RevenueCat. No account is created and we never see a card number.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored, on either platform.

Information We Process

Location

To show local weather, the app may request your device location (precise or approximate). If you grant permission, your latitude and longitude are sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to our weather provider (see below) solely to retrieve a forecast for that point. You can instead search for a city by name; that search text is sent to a geocoding service to resolve coordinates. Location data is used only for app functionality. It is never used for advertising or tracking, and we do not store it on any server of ours — we have none.

This is the same on both platforms. On Android the permission is ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION; on iPhone and iPad it is "While Using the App" location access (NSLocationWhenInUseUsageDescription). Neither version asks for background or "always" location.

Photos

The app lets you pick or capture images for your wardrobe. These images are stored locally on your device only. They are never transmitted off the device, uploaded, or shared. If you choose "Save Image" from the share sheet, the image is written to your own photo library on your device and nowhere else.

App Settings

Preferences (units, theme, character choice, notification choices, selected location) are saved locally on your device using standard on-device storage — SharedPreferences on Android, UserDefaults on iPhone and iPad. They are not sent anywhere.

Home Screen Widgets and Background Refresh

Both the Android and the iOS version offer home screen widgets. To keep a widget current, the app may refresh the forecast in the background — through Android's WorkManager or iOS's background app refresh — which sends the same location to the same weather provider described above, and nothing else. The result is written to storage shared between the app and its widget on your device (a shared preferences file on Android, an App Group container on iPhone and iPad). Remove the widget, or turn off background refresh in your system settings, and these refreshes stop.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, notifications are scheduled and shown locally on your device. There is no push server: no notification data leaves your device, on either platform. Android 13 and later ask for the POST_NOTIFICATIONS permission; iPhone and iPad ask through the standard system notification prompt.

Crash Diagnostics

If the app crashes or hits an unexpected error, a diagnostic report may be sent to our error-monitoring provider (Sentry) so the fault can be fixed. A report contains the error, a stack trace, and basic device information such as the operating system version and device model. It does not contain your name, email, account identifier, advertising ID, photos, or your coordinates — latitude and longitude are stripped from any address recorded in a report before it is sent. These reports are used only to diagnose faults, never for advertising, profiling, or analytics. This applies identically to the Android and iOS versions.

Advertising

WeatherDrobe does not show ads, on Android or on iPhone and iPad. No advertising SDK is initialised, no ad is ever requested, and nothing in the app reads your advertising identifier — Android's Advertising ID or Apple's IDFA. There is no ad-consent form and no App Tracking Transparency prompt, because there is nothing to consent to. If that ever changes, this policy will be updated to describe it before the change ships.

Purchases

WeatherDrobe Premium is optional. If you buy it, the payment itself is handled entirely by the store your device uses — Google Play on Android, the Apple App Store on iPhone and iPad. Your payment details are entered in the store's own checkout sheet and never pass through this app. To know whether Premium is active, the purchase record is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to our billing provider (RevenueCat), which stores it so your purchase can be restored if you reinstall or change device.

This does not create an account. Your purchase is linked to an anonymous identifier generated on your device, not to a name, email address, or login. We never receive or store your card number, bank details, or billing address. If you never buy Premium, nothing is sent to RevenueCat at all.

Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled. You can cancel at any time — on Android in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions, on iPhone and iPad in Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. Cancelling is handled by Google or Apple, not by us.

Third-Party Services

The app sends requests to the following providers, and the list is the same on Android and on iPhone and iPad. We share only what each one needs to answer:

  • Open-Meteo — weather forecasts and city search (open-meteo.com). Receives the coordinates you are asking about, or the city-name text you type. See open-meteo.com for their terms.
  • Sentry — crash and error diagnostics (sentry.io). Receives crash reports as described above. See sentry.io/privacy for their policy.
  • Google Play Billing (Android only) — processes the payment if you buy Premium. Receives your payment details directly; we never see them. Governed by Google's own privacy policy.
  • Apple App Store (iPhone and iPad only) — processes the payment if you buy Premium. Receives your payment details directly; we never see them. Governed by Apple's own privacy policy.
  • RevenueCat — confirms and restores purchases (revenuecat.com). Receives the purchase record and an anonymous install identifier, only if you buy Premium. See revenuecat.com/privacy for their policy.
  • Superwall — presents the Premium offer screen in builds where it is enabled (superwall.com). Receives an anonymous install identifier and basic device and locale information in order to choose which offer to show. It is never contacted if Premium is not offered on your build.

Refer to their policies for how they handle request data. No advertising network is on this list, because the app serves no ads.

Data Sharing and Sale

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it for advertising. We share it only with the providers listed above, and only the minimum each needs. Every one of them acts on our behalf, processing what we send in order to answer us.

Data Security

All network requests use encrypted HTTPS connections. We run no servers of our own, and your settings, photos and wardrobe never leave your device. The only thing held off your device is a purchase receipt at our billing provider, if you bought Premium — and it contains no name, email or payment details. Nothing you typed or photographed in the app is held anywhere but your device.

Data Retention and Deletion

All app data — settings, photos and your wardrobe — lives only on your device. To delete it, uninstall the app (Android or iPhone and iPad alike); we hold no copy, because we store nothing on our side.

If you bought Premium, the purchase receipt held by our billing provider is the one exception. It is what makes your purchase restorable, so it outlives an uninstall by design. To have it erased, email us at the address below and we will request its deletion — note that doing so gives up the ability to restore the purchase. Payment records held by Google Play or Apple are governed by their own policies and retention rules.

Children's Privacy

WeatherDrobe is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Both versions request the same things; only the names differ.

Your Rights

Because we hold no personal data on any server of ours, there is nothing for us to look up, export, or correct about you. Deleting the app deletes everything it stored. The single exception is the purchase receipt described under Data Retention and Deletion, which we will ask our billing provider to erase on request. If you are in a region with statutory data rights (such as the EEA, the UK, or California), write to us at the address below and we will answer.
