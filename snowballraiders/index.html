Snowball Raiders Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026 · Applies to Snowball Raiders on Android and iOS

The short version Snowball Raiders does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal information. The game works fully offline. Your progress is saved only on your own device.

Who we are

Snowball Raiders (the "Game") is developed and published by LogicStack ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the Game handles information when you play it on Android or iOS.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The Game has no user accounts, no sign-in, no chat and no way to enter personal details. It does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, location or any other personal data on your device.

Game progress saved on your device

To keep your progress between sessions, the Game saves a small file in its own private storage on your device. This file contains only game data, such as:

  • Snowflake coins and permanent upgrades you have unlocked
  • Game statistics, such as the highest room reached and runs completed
  • Your settings, such as sound, music, vibration and damage numbers

This file never leaves your device and is never sent to us or to anyone else. You can delete it at any time by using the reset option in the Game's settings, by clearing the app's data, or by uninstalling the Game.

No internet connection, ads or analytics

The Game does not connect to the internet. It contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, no third-party software development kits that collect data, and no social media features. We do not track you across apps or websites.

In-game purchases

Upgrades in the Game are bought only with snowflake coins earned by playing. The Game does not offer any purchases with real money.

Device permissions

On Android, the Game requests only the vibration permission, used to make your phone buzz briefly during gameplay. You can turn vibration off in the Game's settings at any time. The Game requests no other permissions.

App stores

You download the Game through Google Play or the Apple App Store. Google and Apple may collect information, for example to process downloads, provide app updates or create crash and performance reports, under their own privacy policies. We do not control that collection. Any crash or performance reports they share with developers are anonymized and do not identify you. Please review the Google Privacy Policy and the Apple Privacy Policy for details.

Children's privacy

The Game is suitable for players of all ages. Because the Game collects no personal information from anyone, it does not collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country). Parents and guardians can contact us with any questions.

Data security and retention

Because we do not collect or store your data, there is nothing on our side to secure, keep or delete. Your saved game progress stays on your device until you delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the GDPR or the CCPA to access or delete personal information. Since we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing for us to provide or erase, but you are welcome to contact us with any request or question.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the Game handles information, for example by adding new features, we will update this policy and change the effective date above. Significant changes will be noted in the Game's store listing.
