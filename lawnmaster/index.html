Privacy Policy — Lawn Master

Last updated: 21 July 2026

Lawn Master (“the app”), developed by LogicStack (“we”, “us”), is a single-player, offline game. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Data we collect

None. Lawn Master does not collect, store, or transmit any personal or device data. The app has no internet permission and makes no network connections.

Local data

Your game progress (levels, coins, upgrades, and settings) is saved only on your device. It never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the app.

Ads and tracking

The app contains no advertising and uses no advertising identifiers. It includes no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. No data is shared with anyone.

Children

The app collects no data from any user, including children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted at this URL.
