Fitness RPG Privacy Policy

For Fitness RPG on iPhone (iOS) and Android · Effective November 18, 2026

Short version: Fitness RPG works entirely on your device. We do not collect, store, sell, or share your personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, and no tracking. Your workouts stay on your phone. Fitness RPG Pro is an optional purchase on Android; Google Play takes the payment, and we never see your card details.

1. Who we are

Fitness RPG (“the app”) is a fitness role-playing game made by LogicStack (“we”, “us”). This policy explains how the app handles information on both iPhone and Android.

2. Information the app stores on your device

To run the game, the app saves the following inside its private storage on your device:

WhatWhy
Hero name you chooseShown in the game
Workout sets you log (exercise, weight, reps, date and time)Track your history and power your hero’s progress
Custom exercises you addSo you can log them again
Game progress (level, XP, heroes unlocked, bosses defeated)Save your game
Workout plans you makeSo you can run them again
Settings (for example, kilograms or pounds)Remember your preferences
Whether your Google account owns Fitness RPG Pro (Android)Keep Pro unlocked, including when you are offline

This information never leaves your device through the app. We cannot see it, and it is not sent to us or to anyone else. You don’t need to enter your real name; any hero name works.

3. Information we do not collect

  • No account, sign-in, email address, or phone number.
  • No location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone access.
  • No access to Apple Health, Google Health Connect, or other health platforms.
  • No advertising, advertising identifiers, or cross-app tracking.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • No payment information. If you buy Fitness RPG Pro, Google Play takes the payment. We never see your card or billing details.

4. Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your information with anyone. The app never sends us what it stores. The only records we receive are the order records Google Play gives a seller, described in section 6.

5. Third-party technology

Fitness RPG is built with the Unity game engine. The app does not use Unity’s analytics, advertising, cloud, or crash-reporting services. Like most apps, it is downloaded through the Apple App Store or Google Play, which handle your download and any reviews under their own privacy policies:

  • Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

6. Fitness RPG Pro purchases

Fitness RPG Pro is optional. On Android it is sold through Google Play Billing as a monthly or yearly subscription, or as a one-time purchase. Everything else in the app works without it.

  • Google handles the payment. You pay with the payment method on your Google account. Google processes the purchase, the renewals and any refund under the Google Privacy Policy. We never see your card or bank details.
  • What the app does. To unlock Pro, the app asks Google Play on your phone whether your Google account owns it. It gets back which product you own and nothing else, and keeps that answer on your device. The app does not send it to us.
  • What we receive as the seller. Google Play gives us a record of each order: its order number, date, product, price, and the buyer’s general location (such as country and postal code), which is used for tax. We use these records only for accounting, tax, and to help with a refund or support request you send us.
  • Cancelling. A subscription renews until you cancel it. Cancel any time in the Google Play app under Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

7. Device backups

Depending on your phone’s settings, your operating system may include the app’s saved file in your own device backups (such as iCloud Backup on iPhone or Google backup on Android). Those backups are controlled by you and by Apple or Google, not by us.

8. Keeping or deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you remove it. To delete all of it:

iPhone

Press and hold the Fitness RPG icon, tap Remove App, then Delete App. Or go to Settings → General → iPhone Storage → Fitness RPG → Delete App.

Android

Go to Settings → Apps → Fitness RPG → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app.

Because we hold no copy, we cannot recover deleted data for you. Fitness RPG Pro belongs to your Google account, so it comes back when you install the app again and tap Restore purchases.

9. Security

Your saved file sits in the app’s private storage, which your phone’s operating system protects from other apps. Keeping your device locked with a passcode helps keep it safe.

10. Children

Fitness RPG is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). Since the app collects no personal information, it does not collect information from children.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK or the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information a company holds about you. The app sends us none. You can view, change, or delete everything it stores at any time using the steps above. If you bought Fitness RPG Pro, the order records in section 6 are held by Google and by us; contact us to ask about them, and see Google’s policy for the information Google holds.

12. Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes how information is handled (for example, adding online features), we will update this page and change the effective date above before that version is released.
