Privacy Policy for Bhabhi Card Game

Last updated: 20 August 2026

Logicstack LLC ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Bhabhi Card Game mobile application (the "App") for iOS and Android. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and how you can delete it.

The short version. Bhabhi has no sign-up. You never give us your name, email address, or phone number. The App creates an anonymous ID so your coins and level survive reinstalls and so online matches work. You can delete everything from inside the App at any time: Settings → Account → Delete my account and data.

Information We Collect

Anonymous Account ID

The first time you play online, the App creates an anonymous account through Firebase Authentication. This is a random identifier. There is no sign-in screen, and the identifier is not linked to your Apple ID, Google account, email address, or phone number. We cannot use it to identify you personally.

Your Player Name

The name you choose is shown to other players at online tables and on the public leaderboard. Please choose a name you are comfortable showing publicly. Names are screened automatically, and you can report an offensive name at kernelkrafttech@gmail.com.

Game Progress

Coins, XP and level, online rating, achievements, quests, match results and game statistics, stored against your anonymous ID so your progress follows you across devices and reinstalls.

Feedback You Choose to Send

If you submit a note through the rating prompt, we store its text along with your anonymous ID and the platform you sent it from. Please do not include personal details in it.

Standard Technical Data

Google Firebase logs ordinary service data, such as IP address, timestamps, and device and operating system type, when the App communicates with our servers. Firebase processes this on our behalf.

We do not collect your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or health data. The App has no chat feature.

Advertising

On iOS: this version of Bhabhi shows no advertisements. We do not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA), we do not ask for tracking permission, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

On Android: Bhabhi shows Google AdMob advertisements, which you may choose to watch in exchange for in-game coins. AdMob may use an advertising identifier to limit repeat ads, measure performance, and, depending on your settings and region, personalise advertising. Google's handling of that data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Advertisements are never required in order to play the game.

Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland may be shown a consent message to manage advertising choices where required by law.

If we add advertising to the iOS version in a future release, we will update this policy and ask for your permission first through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt.

Purchases

Bhabhi has no in-app purchases. All coins are earned by playing. No real money changes hands anywhere in the App, and we never see or store payment information.

How We Use Your Information

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the App
  • Enable online multiplayer gameplay and matchmaking
  • Save and sync your game progress
  • Track results, statistics, rankings, and the leaderboard
  • Show advertisements and measure their performance (Android only)
  • Detect and prevent cheating, fraud, and abuse
  • Fix crashes, bugs, and technical problems
  • Comply with legal obligations and platform requirements

What We Never Do

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it with data brokers. We do not use it to build advertising profiles about you, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Third-Party Services

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions) for anonymous accounts, game data, and the leaderboard. Firebase privacy
  • Google AdMob for advertising, Android only. AdMob privacy

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on Google Cloud servers and on your own device, and may be processed outside your country, including in the United States. We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect it. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data Retention and Deletion

We keep your data until you delete it. Inactive anonymous accounts may be removed after an extended period of inactivity.

To delete everything from inside the App: Settings → Account → Delete my account and data.

This permanently removes your player name and avatar, coins, level and XP, online rating and leaderboard entry, achievements, quests and statistics, along with the anonymous account itself and your progress on the device. It cannot be undone.

You may also request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data by emailing kernelkrafttech@gmail.com. Because accounts are anonymous, we may be unable to locate your data from an email address alone, so the in-app deletion is the reliable route.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at kernelkrafttech@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of your data. The in-app deletion exercises the deletion right immediately. For anything else, contact us at the address below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will revise the "Last updated" date above, and significant changes will be announced in the App.
