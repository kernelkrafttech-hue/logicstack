Privacy Policy for Bhabi - Card Game

Last updated: May 6, 2026

Logicstack LLC ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Bhabi - Card Game mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our App.

Information We Collect

Account Information: When you sign in, we collect your display name, email address, and a unique user ID through Firebase Authentication. This is required to enable multiplayer functionality.

Gameplay Data: We collect information related to your gameplay, including game results, match history, in-game statistics, and friend connections, in order to provide multiplayer features and matchmaking.

Device Information: We automatically collect limited device information such as device type, operating system version, and app version for crash reporting and performance monitoring through Firebase services.

In-App Purchases: If you make purchases within the App, the transaction is processed by Google Play. We do not collect or store your payment information. We only receive confirmation that a purchase was completed.

How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and maintain multiplayer game functionality
  • Enable account creation, sign-in, and matchmaking
  • Sync your game progress across devices
  • Improve app performance and fix bugs
  • Communicate important updates about the App

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with the following service providers:

  • Google Firebase for Authentication, Firestore, and Cloud Functions
  • Google Play Services for in-app purchases and app distribution

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely on Google Firebase servers. We use industry-standard security measures to protect your information, but no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Your Rights

You may request to access, update, or delete your personal data at any time by contacting us at the email below. You can also delete your account directly within the App, which will remove your associated game data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
