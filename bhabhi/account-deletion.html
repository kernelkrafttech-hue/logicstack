Account and Data Deletion — Bhabi - Card Game

Effective date: May 11, 2026

Bhabi - Card Game is published by LogicStack. This page explains how users can request deletion of their account and associated app data.

Request account deletion

To request deletion of your Bhabi account and associated data, email us at kernelkrafttech@gmail.com with the subject line Bhabi Account Deletion Request.

Please include the email address or sign-in method used for your Bhabi account so we can identify the correct account. Do not send passwords or sensitive information.

What data is deleted

When your request is processed, we delete account-related data associated with your Bhabi account, including where applicable:

  • Your app account/authentication record.
  • Cloud-saved profile or gameplay data linked to your account.
  • Settings, progress, stats, or similar app data stored on our servers and linked to your account.

What data may be kept

We may retain limited records if required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, dispute resolution, or legitimate business records. Any retained data is kept only for as long as necessary for those purposes.

Data stored only on your device can be removed by deleting the app or clearing the app's storage from your Android settings.

Advertising data handled by third-party ad providers, such as Google AdMob, is controlled by those providers and subject to their own policies. You can review Google's privacy controls at Google's Privacy Policy.

Processing time

We aim to process deletion requests within 30 days. We may contact you if we need more information to verify or complete the request.

Contact

For account deletion or privacy questions, contact kernelkrafttech@gmail.com.
