Privacy Policy

Easy Controls Car Game · Last updated: June 20, 2026

Short version: Easy Controls Car Game does not collect, store, or share any personal data. It contains no ads, no analytics, and no third-party tracking. Everything the app saves stays on your device.

This Privacy Policy explains how Easy Controls Car Game ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") handles your information. By using the App, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. The App has no user accounts, no login or sign-in, and does not ask you for your name, email, phone number, location, or any other personal details. We do not use cookies, advertising identifiers, or analytics tools of any kind.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

The App saves a small amount of information locally on your device so it can remember your preferences between sessions, including:

  • Your game settings (such as your selected car, chosen map, and audio preferences)
  • Your high scores

This information is stored only on your device and is never transmitted to us or any third party. It is removed if you uninstall the App or clear its data.

3. Local Multiplayer

The App includes an optional local multiplayer mode that works over your local Wi-Fi network. When you play multiplayer, a display name you choose and your in-game score are shared directly with other players on the same local network so the App can show a shared leaderboard during the match.

  • This data is exchanged directly between devices on the same network.
  • It is not sent to any server, and it is not stored or collected by us.
  • It exists only for the duration of the match and is not retained afterward.
  • There is no chat, messaging, or voice communication in the App.

The App requests Internet/network access on your device solely to enable this local Wi-Fi multiplayer feature.

4. Advertising

The App contains no advertisements and uses no advertising networks or advertising identifiers.

5. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party SDKs, analytics services, social networks, or cloud services. No data is shared with third parties.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for players aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children. Because the App collects no personal data at all, no personal information from children is ever gathered, stored, or shared.

7. Security

Because the App does not collect or transmit personal data to any server, there is no personal data held by us that could be exposed. Locally stored game data remains under your control on your own device.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
