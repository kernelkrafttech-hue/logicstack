Privacy Policy

Easy Controls Car Game · Last updated: (set this on the day it goes live)

Short version: we do not collect or store any personal data ourselves, and the app has no accounts, no sign-in and no analytics. It does show adverts, supplied by Google AdMob, and AdMob collects some data (including your device's advertising ID) to serve them. Everything the game itself saves — your cars, coins and scores — stays on your device.

This Privacy Policy explains how Easy Controls Car Game ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") handles your information. By using the App, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information We Collect

We collect no personal information ourselves. The App has no user accounts, no login or sign-in, and never asks you for your name, email, phone number or location. We operate no servers and use no analytics.

The App does show adverts, and our advertising partner collects data in order to serve them. See section 4 for exactly what that covers and how to limit it.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

The App saves a small amount of information locally on your device so it can remember your preferences between sessions, including:

  • Your game settings (such as your selected car, chosen map, and audio preferences)
  • Your high scores

This information is stored only on your device and is never transmitted to us or any third party. It is removed if you uninstall the App or clear its data.

3. Local Multiplayer

The App includes an optional local multiplayer mode that works over your local Wi-Fi network. When you play multiplayer, a display name you choose and your in-game score are shared directly with other players on the same local network so the App can show a shared leaderboard during the match.

  • This data is exchanged directly between devices on the same network.
  • It is not sent to any server, and it is not stored or collected by us.
  • It exists only for the duration of the match and is not retained afterward.
  • There is no chat, messaging, or voice communication in the App.

The App requests Internet/network access on your device solely to enable this local Wi-Fi multiplayer feature.

4. Advertising

The App shows adverts supplied by Google AdMob (Google LLC). There are two kinds, and neither one interrupts play at random:

  • Rewarded videos you choose to watch. Nothing plays until you tap a button that says what you get for it — bonus coins, or another go at a run you just lost.
  • One occasional full-screen advert shown while leaving a multiplayer or arena results screen.

To serve and measure those adverts, AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device and operating-system details, and information about advert interactions. That data is collected by Google under its own privacy policy, not by us, and we never receive it in a form that identifies you.

  • Google's policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Your choices. Android and iOS both let you reset or delete your advertising ID, and opt out of personalised advertising, in the device's own privacy settings. If you are in the EU, the UK or Switzerland, the App asks for your consent before requesting its first advert, and you can change that answer at any time from the game's Settings screen.

The App also offers a one-off purchase, Skip the Ads. With it, no adverts are ever requested or shown — every bonus that a video would have paid is granted instantly instead.

5. Third-Party Services

The App integrates two third-party services, and no others — no analytics, no social networks, no cloud storage:

  • Google AdMob — serves the adverts described in section 4.
  • Google Play Billing (Android) / the App Store (iOS) — processes the Skip the Ads purchase. We never see or receive your payment details; the store tells the App only whether the item is owned.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for players aged 13 and older, and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Adverts are requested with Google's child-directed and under-age-of-consent settings applied where the store listing or your declared age requires them, which restricts them to non-personalised advertising.

7. Security

We hold no personal data of yours on any server, so there is none for us to lose. Locally stored game data stays under your control on your own device. Data collected by Google for advertising and payments is held and protected by Google under its own policies.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
