Privacy Policy

Little Stack: Pre-K ABC & Math

Effective date: November 18, 2026
Publisher: Logicstack LLC (“we”, “us”)

Little Stack is a learning app for children ages 2 to 5. We built it so that we never receive any information about you or your child. This policy applies to Little Stack wherever it is offered, including Android devices (Google Play) and iPhone and iPad (Apple App Store).

The short version

  • We do not collect any personal information.
  • The app works without the internet and sends nothing to us or to anyone else.
  • There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no accounts and no in-app purchases.
  • Everything the app saves stays on your device, and a grown-up can delete it at any time.

What the app saves on your device

The app saves the following only on your device, in the app’s private storage:

  • Player profiles: a nickname (chosen from a list, or typed by a grown-up behind the parental gate) and a picture of an animal buddy.
  • Learning progress: which lessons each player has finished, how many answers were right or needed another try, and when a lesson was last played.
  • Settings: sound effects, spoken instructions and voice speed.

We do not ask for real names, birthdates, photos, email addresses, location or any other personal information.

What the app does not do

  • It does not connect to the internet. On Android, the app does not even request the internet permission.
  • It does not collect, send, share or sell any data, to us or to anyone else.
  • It has no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, no accounts and no in-app purchases, and it contains no third-party advertising or analytics software.
  • It does not use the advertising ID or any other device identifier, and it does not track you across other apps or websites.
  • It does not use the camera, microphone, contacts, photos or location.
  • It has no chat, no social features and no links that a child can follow out of the app.

Spoken instructions

The app speaks using audio recorded inside the app or your device’s built-in text-to-speech voice. To read a line aloud, the app hands the words (which can include a player’s nickname) to that voice. The voice is part of your device’s operating system and is provided by Apple, Google or your device’s maker. We receive nothing.

Backups

  • Android: cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are turned off for this app, so its data is not copied to a backup service.
  • iPhone and iPad: if you choose to back up your device with iCloud or a computer, the app’s saved data may be included in your own backup. That backup belongs to you and is handled by Apple under your settings. We never have access to it.

Children’s privacy

Little Stack is made for children under 13 and their families. Because we do not collect personal information from anyone, there is nothing for us to access, use, share, sell or delete.

We designed the app to follow the U.S. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the Google Play Families Policy and Apple’s App Store rules for apps made for kids.

If you live somewhere with privacy laws such as the GDPR (Europe and the United Kingdom) or the CCPA (California): we do not collect, sell or share your personal information, so we hold no data about you or your child to provide, correct or delete.

Deleting data

A grown-up can reset a player’s progress or delete a player in the parent area (press and hold the lock, then answer a question). Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data from the device.

Links and this web page

The parent area contains one link, to this online copy of the policy. It sits behind the parental gate and opens your web browser.

This web page uses no cookies and no tracking scripts. The company that hosts the page may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses), as any website does.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and the copy inside the app.
