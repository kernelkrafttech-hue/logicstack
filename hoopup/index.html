Hoop Up! Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 25, 2026 · Developer: LogicStack · Applies to Hoop Up! on Android (Google Play) and iOS (Apple App Store)

The short version

Hoop Up! does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information. The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no internet connection. Your progress is saved only on your own device.

1. Who we are

Hoop Up! (“the app”, “the game”) is a single-player arcade basketball game made by LogicStack (“we”, “us”). This policy explains how the app handles information. If you have questions, contact us at CONTACT_EMAIL.

2. Information we collect

None. We do not collect any personal or device information from you, and the app does not send any data to us or to anyone else.

3. Game data stored on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves a small file on your device. It contains only game information, such as:

  • coins, XP and level
  • unlocked characters, upgrades and cosmetic items
  • career, tournament and match statistics
  • daily challenge progress
  • settings such as sound volume, vibration and difficulty

This file never leaves your device through the app, and we cannot see or access it. Coins and items in the game are earned by playing. They have no real-money value and cannot be bought.

If you have turned on your device’s own backup service (such as iCloud Backup or Google backup), that service may include the game’s save file in your device backup. Those backups are handled by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies, not by us.

4. Permissions

AndroidThe app requests only the vibrate permission, used for haptic feedback during play. You can turn vibration off in the game’s settings. The app does not request internet access.

iOSThe app does not request access to your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or tracking.

5. Advertising, tracking and third parties

  • The app shows no ads.
  • The app does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites, as defined by Apple’s App Tracking Transparency.
  • The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • Because we collect nothing, we do not sell or share any personal information with anyone.

Apple and Google may collect information about your download and use of their app stores, and, if you have chosen to share diagnostics with them in your device settings, may provide developers with anonymous, aggregated crash and performance reports. That collection is governed by the Apple Privacy Policy and the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Children’s privacy

Hoop Up! is suitable for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). Because the app collects no data at all, no personal information from children is collected, used or disclosed. This is consistent with the U.S. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the GDPR.

7. Data retention and deletion

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete. Your game save stays on your device until you remove it:

  • AndroidUninstall the app, or go to Settings → Apps → Hoop Up! → Storage → Clear storage.
  • iOSDelete the app from your device. To remove it from backups, manage your iCloud Backup in Settings.

8. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the EU/UK GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) and similar laws give you rights to access, correct, delete or move your personal data, to object to processing, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal data. Because we do not collect or hold any personal data, we have none to provide, correct, delete or sell. You can still contact us at any time with questions, and we will reply.

9. Security

The app does not transmit any data, so your information is never exposed in transit or stored on our servers. Your save file is protected by your device’s own security, such as your screen lock and the operating system’s app sandbox.

10. Changes to this policy

If a future version of Hoop Up! adds features that handle personal data, such as online play, accounts, ads or purchases, we will update this policy before releasing that version, change the effective date above, and update the app’s Google Play Data safety section and App Store privacy details.
