Why it matters

A slow network and clunky software cost you more than you think.

These are the things that quietly drain time and money until someone with the right engineering background fixes them for good.

🐌

Slow, flaky network

Dropped Wi-Fi, dead zones, sluggish connections, the system that "just goes down sometimes." It kills productivity and nobody can tell you why.

🔓

Wide-open security

Flat network, default passwords, no segmentation, no firewall rules worth the name. One bad click and the whole shop is exposed.

🧩

Software that doesn't fit

You pay monthly for five tools that half-work, don't talk to each other, and force your team into workarounds and copy-paste between tabs.
