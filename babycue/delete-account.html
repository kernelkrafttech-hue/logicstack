Delete your account

Last updated: September 22, 2026

This page explains how to delete your BabyCue account and the data linked to it. BabyCue is a baby tracking app made by LogicStack. These steps apply to the app on Android (Google Play) and on iPhone and iPad (App Store). You don't need the app installed to ask for deletion.

The short version

  • If you have the app: open BabyCue, tap More, then Delete Account. Deletion happens straight away.
  • If you don't have the app: email support@babycue.app from the email address on your account. We delete it within 30 days.
  • Deleting your account does not cancel a Premium subscription. Cancel it with Google Play or Apple as described below.

Option 1: Delete from inside the app

The steps are the same on both platforms.

Android

  1. Open BabyCue and sign in.
  2. Tap the More tab at the bottom.
  3. Under Account, tap Delete Account.
  4. Read what will be deleted, type DELETE, and tap Delete my account.

iPhone and iPad

  1. Open BabyCue and sign in.
  2. Tap the More tab at the bottom.
  3. Under Account, tap Delete Account.
  4. Read what will be deleted, type DELETE, and tap Delete my account.

Your account and data are removed right away and you are signed out. Deleting your account from one device deletes it everywhere.

Option 2: Ask us by email

Use this if you've uninstalled BabyCue, lost your phone, or can't sign in.

  1. Send an email to support@babycue.app with the subject "Delete my BabyCue account".
  2. Send it from the email address you use to sign in to BabyCue. If you signed in with Google, use that Google address. If you used Sign in with Apple with "Hide My Email", tell us the relay address (it ends in @privaterelay.appleid.com) or send from it.
  3. We may reply to confirm it's you before deleting anything.
  4. We complete the deletion within 30 days and email you when it's done.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile, email address, display name and sign-in record.
  • Every baby you own, with all its entries (feeds, sleep, diapers, pumping, medicine, growth, temperature, activities and notes), milestones, reminders, photos and invitations.
  • Your membership in babies that other people own. You lose access to them.
  • Invitations sent to your email address that you never accepted.
  • Your push-notification tokens and your subscription status in BabyCue.

What we keep, and for how long

  • Entries you logged in someone else's baby stay in that family's record, marked with the display name you used. They belong to that family's care record, not to your account.
  • Invitations you accepted into someone else's baby stay as a record that someone joined, but your email address is removed from them.
  • Backups held by our cloud provider are overwritten on a rolling cycle within 30 days.
  • Anonymous usage counts are kept for up to 2 months. They were never linked to your care data.
  • Crash reports follow our provider's own retention schedule and contain no care data.
  • Purchase records held by RevenueCat, Google Play and Apple are kept for refunds, restores and tax rules. Ask us by email if you want the RevenueCat records deleted as well.

Cancel your subscription first

Google and Apple bill Premium subscriptions, not BabyCue. Deleting your account doesn't stop the billing.

Android

  1. Open the Google Play Store.
  2. Tap your profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions.
  3. Select BabyCue and tap Cancel subscription.

iPhone and iPad

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap your name, then Subscriptions.
  3. Select BabyCue and tap Cancel Subscription.

Guest accounts

If you used "Continue as guest", your account has no email address, so we can't confirm by email that it's yours. Delete a guest account from inside the app (Option 1) while you still have it. If you've already signed out or uninstalled, the data can't be linked back to you, and we can't find it or remove it on request.

Data on your device

  • Deleting your account clears BabyCue's copy of your data on the device you use, and clears its home-screen widgets.
  • Settings about the phone itself, such as theme, units and clock format, stay until you uninstall BabyCue.
  • PDF reports or exported JSON/CSV files you saved or shared are outside BabyCue. Delete those yourself.
  • Sign in with Apple: to also stop sharing your Apple ID with BabyCue, open iPhone Settings, tap your name, then Sign in with Apple, select BabyCue, and tap Stop Using Apple ID.
  • Google sign-in: to remove BabyCue's access to your Google account, go to myaccount.google.com/connections and remove BabyCue.
