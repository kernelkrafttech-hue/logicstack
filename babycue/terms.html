The service

BabyCue is a personal record-keeping app for tracking a baby's feeding, sleep, diapers, growth and related routine, with optional reminders, pattern insights and caregiver sharing. It is available for iPhone and iPad on the Apple App Store and for Android on Google Play. These terms apply to both.

BabyCue is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Predictions and cues are estimates based on your own entries. Always talk to your pediatrician or another qualified professional about your child's health, and call emergency services in an emergency.
